# World Economy Flow data

The navigator is independent of the ladder engine and does not place trades. The screen shows market strength as price performance.

## Market strength

The five top-level bubbles show price performance for explicitly labeled representatives: S&P 500, BND, EUR/USD, GLD and BTC/USDT. They are individual index, ETF, currency-pair or coin proxies, not whole-market strength, turnover or capital flow. The 24H, 7D, 30D and 90D filters select a lookback; source reporting periods are displayed and may differ. Binance provides completed four-hour candles. FRED provides daily observations that may skip weekends and holidays. Finnhub quotes compare the latest price with the previous close and are available only under 24H; they do not represent a rolling 24-hour return. API availability, account entitlements, data delays and geographic restrictions can affect results.

Prices, trading volume, market capitalization and cross-border flows are different measures. Do not infer a transfer from one market into another from these figures.
